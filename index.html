Know before you go

Straight answers, no lectures. Guides live here as we publish them.

Start low, go slow

Begin with a small amount, wait to feel the full effect, and only then decide whether you want more. Edibles can take two hours or longer to kick in.

Know your local laws

Cannabis rules differ by state and country, including where you can buy, possess, and consume. Check your local regulations before you travel.

Consume responsibly

Never drive after consuming, keep products locked away from kids and pets, and skip it if you're pregnant or on medication that could interact.
